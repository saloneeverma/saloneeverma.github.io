I'm a student researcher interested in exploring natural and artificial intelligence through either neuroscience-inspired AI or AI-inspired neuroscience. For now, this looks like RL for [biomechanical body models of _Drosophila melanogaster_](https://github.com/TuragaLab/flybody) in the [Turaga Lab](https://www.janelia.org/lab/turaga-lab) at [HHMI Janelia](https://www.hhmi.org/research/janelia), where I am grateful to be supervised by [Dr. Srinivas Turaga](https://www.janelia.org/people/srinivas-turaga) and have been mentored by Dr. Gert-Jan Both, Dr. Mason McGill, and Justin Ellis-Joyce. 

At the [University of Virginia](https://www.virginia.edu/), where I'm a [Jefferson Scholar](https://www.jeffersonscholars.org/scholarships) studying [Computer Science (concentrating in AI)](https://engineering.virginia.edu/department/computer-science) and [Cognitive Science (concentrating in Linguistics)](https://cogsci.as.virginia.edu/), I work in human-AI collaboration and cognitive psychology. Currently, I'm an undergraduate research assistant in the [Human-AI Technology Lab](https://hai-lab-uva.github.io/) with [Dr. Afsaneh Doryab](https://www.afsanehdoryab.com/) and the [Long Term Memory Lab](https://longtermmemorylab.com/) with [Dr. Nicole Long](https://psychology.as.virginia.edu/people/nicole-long).

I am currently pursuing my Distinguished Major Thesis in Computer Science, advised by [Dr. Yangfeng Ji](https://engineering.virginia.edu/faculty/yangfeng-ji), focusing on  benchmarking LLMs on abstracted grammatical reasoning capabilities using low-resource languages. I am focusing on morphological complexity and typological distinctiveness, and aim to understand the viability of LLM-based revitalization efforts for endangered languages in the Americas.  

At various points during the last eight years I've been working with AI, I've dabbled in machine translation for [speech affected by Broca's aphasia](https://talkbank.org/aphasia/), creating apps for [managing oral allergy syndrome](https://stanfordhealthcare.org/content/dam/SHC/clinics/menlo-medical-clinic/docs/Allergy/Oral%20Allergy%20Syndrome.pdf), developing pipelines for LLM-generated reward functions during RL with [Gert-Jan](https://github.com/GJBoth), and creating CortexConnoisseur, a computational neuroscience LLM research assistant, with [Justin](https://github.com/0xJustin). Other past interests have included control barrier functions, robot autonomy, named entity recognition, and chatbots. I also used to research the philosophical and artistic implications of Modernist (&, more specifically, Imagist) poetry on the efficacy of AI-generated poetry with the Poetry, Ethics, and AI Working Group under [Dr. Justin Stec](https://www.linkedin.com/in/justin-stec-3a484768). 

On the side, I am interested in AI governance and responsible technology policy. My joint independent research project with Hanaan Kazia, "Can Students Regulate AI On Campus? A Case Study", has recently been accepted by the Yale Undergraduate Research Conference 2027. Relatedly, I am a member of the [first student body focused on technology innovation in the United States](https://dtdlab.virginia.edu/project/student-technology-council/), where I consult with administration on technology innovation policy affecting students at UVA, and a [Student AI Guide](https://cte.virginia.edu/initiatives/genai-initiatives#programs) at the UVA Center for Teaching Excellence. 

I also moonlight as the president of [HooHacks](https://team.hoohacks.io/), Virginia's largest hackathon, and organize UVA's [Estimathon](https://estimathon.com/), a team-based contest in collaboration with Jane Street. Outside of academia and policy, the hackathon community is where I invest most of my time. I won a couple hackathons a long time ago (the [2022 NASA App Development Challenge](https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/app-development-challenge/) and the [2022 NASM Air and Space Hackathon](https://airandspace.si.edu/whats-on/events/air-and-space-hackathon)), and I've spent the last five years planning hackathons across Virginia. I also organized the first and (likely) last [Lettuce Eating Competition at UVA at HooHacks 2026](https://hoohacks-2026.devpost.com/). 

I think about AI & its societal implications a lot. Check out my perspective pieces and essays elsewhere on the site. Oh, and I have a small but mighty [Creative Writing](https://creativewriting.virginia.edu/) minor.
