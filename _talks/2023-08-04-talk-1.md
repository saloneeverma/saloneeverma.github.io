I presented my summer research on CortexConnoisseur, a computational neuroscience LLM research assistant, during the 2023 Janelia High School Internship Symposium. I was mentored by Justin Ellis-Joyce and supervised by Dr. Srinivas Turaga. I fine-tuned LLaMA 2 using LoRA on a custom cleaned dataset containing scraped data from academic publications. Our final product was capable of answering questions about computational neuroscience with minimal hallucination and better fine-tuned accuracy, through data mining and cleaning of 19 million academic papers.
